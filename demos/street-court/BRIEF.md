# Street Court demo direction

Scope: independent demo of the four core website areas: home, lineup builder/saved lineup, live rankings, and pregame player intelligence/predicted starters. All data is simulated; no production code or database changes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An outdoor basketball tournament poster becomes a usable fantasy workspace, rather than recoloring only the ranking list.
OWN-WORLD: Asphalt #121210, orange #ff6b2b, uncoated ivory #eeeada. Anton lettering, court markings, jersey geometry, scorekeeper numerals, restrained squared controls.
STORY: Enter the court, research a player, assemble five within $125, save the demo squad, and watch an overtaking sequence.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 92px navigation; desktop split typographic hero/orange basketball geometry, followed by a three-game strip. Other pages use the same navigation and large title, then task-specific workspaces.
FORM: User-assigned street basketball world; code-led, no competing comp or external brand copy. Signature: lineup court interaction and coordinated score/rank/court feedback.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No raster assets. Anton is locally hosted from Google Fonts, licensed under SIL OFL; asset license is retained.
